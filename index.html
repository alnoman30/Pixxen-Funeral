The immediate need path: help for a family right now

The first question on every funeral home website is, “A death has occurred, what do I do?” The answer should be easy to find. We create a calm, consistent immediate-need path on every page with the three key things families need.

Obituaries are the front door of a funeral home website

Obituaries bring many visitors to funeral home websites. They help people find service details, sign the guestbook, send flowers, and learn about the funeral home.

Service Details First

Date, time, location, livestream link, and directions at the top, before obituary. This is what most visitors come for and need right away.

Tribute Wall and Guestbook

Space for condolences, photos, and memories, with moderation so the family is protected while sharing shared stories and respectful memorials.

Flowers and Gifts

A direct link to send flowers or make memorial donations, through your florist partner or an integrated store for a simple and respectful giving experience.

Alerts and Sharing

Obituary notifications by email or SMS, and sharing buttons to send the service details directly to family or social media for easier access and sharing.

Findable by Name

Obituary pages structured and optimized so they rank for the person's name in your town, which is how the funeral home is discovered.

Platform Integration

If you use a case management or obituary platform, the design integrates with or links to it so publishing stays a one-step process for your staff.

  • Cremation services, with and without a ceremony

  • Religious and cultural traditions you serve

Clear and detailed pricing with the general price list

Families compare, quietly, and price is part of it. The FTC Funeral Rule requires funeral homes to provide a General Price List on request, and posting it online is increasingly what families expect. The site includes a clearly designed pricing page with your General Price List, package descriptions and a plain explanation of what affects cost, so a family never feels they had to ask.

If you prefer not to post full pricing, the page presents general ranges and an easy way to request the list. The decision is yours; the structure supports both.

Pre-planning and pre-need, presented without pressure

Pre-planning is a growing part of most funeral homes' work and the least urgent visitor on the site. The pre-planning section is written for someone who is thinking ahead for themselves or a parent: what pre-need arrangements are, how funding works in general terms, and how to start a conversation.

Pre-Planning Vs. Pre-Funding

Clear guidance that helps visitors understand the difference and choose the right financial planning path.

Downloadable Planning Guide
